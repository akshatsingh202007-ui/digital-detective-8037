MISSION 5 – NUMBERS AND DATA

TASK 5.1 – MANUAL CONVERSIONS

1. Decimal 13 to Binary

13 / 2 = 6 remainder 1
6 / 2 = 3 remainder 0
3 / 2 = 1 remainder 1
1 / 2 = 0 remainder 1

Answer: 1101

2. Decimal 25 to Binary

25 / 2 = 12 remainder 1
12 / 2 = 6 remainder 0
6 / 2 = 3 remainder 0
3 / 2 = 1 remainder 1
1 / 2 = 0 remainder 1

Answer: 11001

3. Binary 1010 to Decimal

(1 x 8) + (0 x 4) + (1 x 2) + (0 x 1) = 10

Answer: 10

4. Binary 1101 to Decimal

(1 x 8) + (1 x 4) + (0 x 2) + (1 x 1) = 13

Answer: 13

5. Hexadecimal A to Decimal

A = 10

Answer: 10

6. Hexadecimal 1F to Decimal

(1 x 16) + (15 x 1) = 31

Answer: 31

7. Binary 1111 to Hexadecimal

1111 in binary = 15 in decimal = F in hexadecimal

Answer: F
